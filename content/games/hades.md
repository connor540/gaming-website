# Hades: The Return

Each escape attempt deepens your understanding of the world and the systems surrounding it. The sense of progression is immediate, and the run-to-run adjustments keep the game fresh.

## Highlights

- Distinct weapon classes with meaningful playstyle shifts.
- Rich character writing that makes each encounter memorable.
- Fast, satisfying combat that rewards mastery.

## Conclusion

If you enjoy challenging action games with a strong reward loop, this is a pure example of the genre executed at the highest level.
